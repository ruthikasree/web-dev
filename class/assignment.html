my dream destination

south korea

My dream destination is South Korea, a place I’ve always wanted to visit. I’m especially excited to explore its amazing skincare culture, shop for a wide range of Korean skincare products, discover new beauty trends, and experience the vibrant streets, food, fashion, and beautiful places the country has to offer.

top places to visit in south korea

  1. Seoul – Myeongdong

    • Shop for a huge variety of Korean skincare and beauty products.
    • Explore popular brands like COSRX, Laneige, Beauty of Joseon, and Innisfree.
    • Try different products, collect samples, and enjoy the lively shopping streets.

  2. Seoul – Olive Young

    • Spend time exploring Korea’s famous beauty stores and discovering trending skincare
    • Compare different cleansers, serums, sunscreens, and masks.
    • Pick up Korean beauty products that are difficult or expensive to find elsewhere.

  3. Seoul – Gyeongbokgung Palace

    • Explore the beautiful traditional palace and learn about Korean history.
    • Wear a traditional hanbok and take lots of pictures.
    • Walk around the surrounding traditional streets and cafés.
